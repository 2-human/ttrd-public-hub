Questions from the session

Answered live by the presenter or in the chat by our team. Questions are shared without names.

Which account should I start with?

Many new traders start with the Cent account: positions are 100 times smaller, so you learn with real markets and small amounts. The PRO account suits most traders after that.

Answered live · chapter 26:20
Where do I find my MetaTrader 5 login?

In your client area, under your trading accounts. The login is your trading account number, not your email.

Answered in chat
How much should I risk on a trade?

We can't advise on amounts. Decide a small, fixed share of your balance before every trade and size the position so a stop-loss hit costs no more than that. The risk chapter shows how.

Answered live · chapter 36:05
Can I practise first?

Yes: open a demo account with virtual money, the same platform and the same prices.

Answered in chat
What is leverage?

It lets you control a larger position with a smaller amount. It magnifies gains and losses equally.

Answered in chat
How much can I deposit, and how fast?

From $10, and most payment methods are instant and free. Only deposit what you can afford to lose.
